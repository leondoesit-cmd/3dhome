# Native paper ellipse

Confirmed gap for the ellipse/cylinder construction workflow identified by the parent on PDF 93–94 / printed 2-7–2-8: solver sketches already supported ellipses, but independent native Drawing paper annotations offered only line, polyline, rectangle, circle and three-point arc. Circle arcs are not a substitute for a true ellipse.

Drawing now exposes **Paper ellipse** on the ordinary drawing rail. Pick center, major-axis endpoint, then a minor-radius point. The perpendicular projection of the third pick determines the minor semi-axis. Ctrl bypasses snapping, Shift aligns and Escape cancels. Invalid third picks retain the first two controls for correction. The minor semi-axis cannot exceed the major semi-axis. These are independent paper millimeters, not CAD model units or view-scaled dimensions.

Select and double-click the actual ellipse curve to edit **Center X (paper mm)**, **Center Y (paper mm)**, **Major semi-axis (paper mm)**, **Minor semi-axis (paper mm)**, **Rotation (degrees)** and **Line style**. Semi-axis values are radii, half the overall ellipse width/height in its local axes. Positive rotation is clockwise on the paper because paper Y points down. Exact numeric editing reconstructs the persistent three-point annotation; normal source/history/owner guards and Undo/Redo remain active.

Canvas uses its native ellipse command; shared SVG exports a true `<ellipse>` with analytic center, radii and rotation. Hit testing minimizes distance to the mathematical curve, and paper containment uses exact rotated-ellipse extrema. No polygon, circle-arc or model-feature approximation is used.
