## Shared manual dimension construction

All coordinates below are paper mm, from top-left. A horizontal dimension between featureX=a/b at ordinate yFeature can be drawn at yDim using two extension lines beginning1mm away from the feature and ending2mm beyond yDim, one dimension line with a text gap around its midpoint, two filled/closed small triangular arrowhead polylines, and a note containing the value. A vertical dimension uses the analogous rotated geometry. Arrowheads go between extensions where they fit; dimension text stays horizontal in an unidirectional convention. Avoid dimension lines directly on object boundaries, duplicate closed chains and extension lines touching the object.
