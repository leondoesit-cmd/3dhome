The protected original export remains diagnostic and is not accepted.

A fresh exact BRep point probe additionally found material at the first segment's bead boundary: source centimeters `(-.05600000000000037,6.359683277095792,1.4799999999999998)`. The center samples remained outside; testing only midpoint centers would miss this defect. These probes are an independent oracle, not authored CAD.

The bounded product fix subtracts each exact contour-edge capsule from a proposed support segment. Endpoint disks and finite edge strips produce excluded parameter intervals; their union is removed, and remaining intervals are rejected when inside material. Classification unions material per object, preserving each object's hole parity. This retains safe support pieces rather than discarding an entire useful line. A final exact contour clearance guard rejects numerical or unhandled failures. No source geometry, slice setting, existing material deposition guard, or raster budget changed. Each trimmed support line adds a diagnostic with removed length and retained piece count.

Implementation: `js/core/print_raster.js` exports `clipSupportPaths` and `supportClearsContours`; `js/core/print_toolpaths.js` applies clipping only to support paths. The clipping contract accepts finite2D coordinates and positive bead radius. Unchanged safe endpoints retain their original numeric values.

## Actual post-fix replay and mesh approximation allowance

Actual Chrome post-fix and its cold export remove the original two collisions and retain4321 support paths. Clipping diagnostics identify layers76 and80; support volume becomes18596.053728012394 mm³. A strict independently remeshed contour check still reports one endpoint-cap clearance of .3990592316509056 mm versus .4 mm bead radius. Exact native BRep probes disambiguate this remeshing discrepancy: the closest-direction .4 mm point is OUT, .401 mm is IN, and all3600 sampled angular points on the actual .4 mm endpoint cap are OUT. This supports a native clearance just above .4 mm but does not change the strict polygon audit threshold. The intermediate artifacts remain qualified diagnostics pending the stronger margin replay.

The bounded refinement passes the actual native mesh's declared `.01 mm` approximation allowance to support clipping. The UI's `.01 mm` deviation becomes `.001 cm` only when passed to kernel meshing; print contours and the allowance use millimeters. The clip uses bead radius plus allowance and records `supportContourAllowanceMm` and the diagnostic margin. It only trims or removes support pieces, preserves all material paths and user slice settings, and leaves the strict audit guard unchanged. This allowance is tied to the actual native FFF preview tessellation, not a universal clearance guarantee for arbitrarily coarse external meshes.
