## Standard constructions: useful, but insufficient to fix every datum

Concentricity can tie the central Ø10 bore, retained circular contour and R25 pocket to one centre. It removes relative centre offsets. It cannot set that centre's longitudinal D station. H35 is supported by the construction height, but D25 and D30 correspond to different existing rail/core tangent stations. Selecting one because the rendered ellipse looks aligned would be a projected-image estimate, not a dimension.

A tangent R20 rear pocket can be constrained with a straight boundary and quarter-circle. If interpreted on the rear W/H face, a circle centred on an outer width datum and H38 creates a natural open quarter-pocket. If interpreted on the side D/H face, an analogous circle centred on a rear-depth datum creates a different pocket. These constructions explain smooth topology but do not prove the sketch plane, cut depth, and hole axis together. The radius annotation alone supplies none of those missing choices.

Likewise the underside20°/45°/R10 construction fixes its tangent profile once the D45/D85 baseline endpoints are accepted. It does not fix the feature's transverse cut extent. The analytical tangent coordinates in the previous witness note remain valid for that stated construction and need no mass fitting.

The top hole's stated W20 follows30 from outer W50. Its20-from-top-front-edge datum still conflicts with the currently accepted core bevel if taken literally: D36.551464+20 givesD56.551464, whose R5 opening crosses the D60 step. A projected un-beveled datum, or a different edge named “front”, would give a different centre. The extra colored view does not supply that missing datum. No bevel change or hole relocation is justified solely to force a closed opening.
