# Exercise 8.20 directed clapper hinge stop

The frozen Shaper Tool Head requires forward clapper lift around its reader-local +X hinge axis, signed −20..0 degrees from closed. Its molded rear striker is a backstop. An unsigned Angle range 0..20 degrees admits both forward and backward orientations and cannot describe this requirement. The existing unsigned Angle and linear Limit semantics remain unchanged.

The new canonical `rotationlimit` mate supplies a signed angular stop between parallel or opposed exact hinge axes. Reference A is the reader/box axis; B is the moving/clapper axis. The directed A axis determines positive rotation; `reverse=true` negates it. Ordinary concentric and axial-capture mates still locate the hinge and provide its other five constraints. A free nonzero range retains one rotational freedom at both stops and within its interior. Equal bounds or a finite `driveAngle` remove that freedom.

Creation captures zero using `rotationMarkerA` and `rotationMarkerB` in the two component-local frames. At evaluation, transported markers are projected into the native hinge plane and measured with signed `atan2` around the current A axis. The angle follows guide feed, common apron yaw and whole-assembly placement; it is independent of world Euler Rx and camera orientation. A native axis may point −X, in which case the actual reference sign is honored and Reverse can select the intended direction.

`angleMin`, `angleMax` and optional `driveAngle` are canonical degrees. Bounds must be finite, ordered and no more than one 360-degree period wide. The periodic representation nearest the interval center lets 170..190 degrees remain continuous through the quaternion ±180-degree wrap. This is a generic signed rotational interval, not a filter on the book's illustrated pose. A passive out-of-range joint settles to its nearest stop. A prescribed moving component beyond a stop fails the physical angular tolerance, and explicit driving values outside the authored range are rejected before mutation.

`js/cad_rotation_limit_mate.js` exports `rotationLimitMateSpec`, `captureRotationLimitZero`, `rotationLimitMeasure` and solver seeding. Seeding turns about the actual hinge line, preserving its pivot rather than rotating a component about an arbitrary document origin. `js/cad_rotation_limit_controls.js` exports transactional creation, settings, Drive and reference replacement helpers. These solve disposable pose/mate copies, publish only successful results and call the undo callback only before commit. Reference replacement retains component roles, mate identity and driving value, recapturing its relative zero at the saved driven pose. Failed specs, skew references and contradictory mate systems publish no diagnostic subset pose or undo entry. Native imports with missing/nonfinite bounds are reported as invalid data rather than silently contributing NaN/zero residuals.
